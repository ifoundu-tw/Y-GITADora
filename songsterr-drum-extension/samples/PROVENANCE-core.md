from one CC0 source. The WAVs under `samples/` are **source, not build

`include_bytes!`.

> **The accent-cymbal banks — `crash`, `splash` and the Karoryfer
> `china` — moved to `ferrosintesis-samples-drumkit2` on 2026-07-25.** The combined
> kit packaged at 15.8 MiB and crates.io rejects any crate over 10 MiB; cymbals hold
> much of the payload, so moving the long banks keeps both packages below the limit.
> Nothing was re-cut or re-processed and renders are
> byte-identical — see that crate's `PROVENANCE.md` for their sources and inventory.
> The ride, ride bell and hi-hats are cymbals too and stayed here: the seam was cut on
> file size, not instrument taxonomy.

## Source (pinned by commit SHA)

### Virtuosity Drums — every bank in this crate

- Repo: <https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums>
- Pinned revision: `9f04cf9a734527edfbb0a4eee1f674e45bbf71bc` (master HEAD at
  fetch time, 2026-07-14)
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal**, verified from the repo's own `LICENSE` file
  at the pinned revision (it is the verbatim Creative Commons legal code,
  beginning "Creative Commons Legal Code / CC0 1.0 Universal"). A verbatim
  copy ships in this crate as `LICENSE-CC0`.
- What it is: a contemporary jazz kit played with sticks by Austin McMahon,
  recorded at Virtuosity Musical Instruments, Boston, by Versilian Studios /
  Karoryfer Samples.
- Mic sets used: **`mid` for every family except the kick; `kickmic` for the
  kick**. Of the seven recorded positions (`kickmic`, `snaremic`, `oh`, `mid`,
  `room`, `lofi`, plus `perc`), `mid` is the balanced mono-friendly middle
  position: more direct than `room`, less close than the spot mics, with no
  lo-fi coloration. The kick uses its dedicated close mic because the `mid`
  captures had little 30–70 Hz energy and sounded boxy; `kickmic` preserves the
  low end while retaining the same 4×4 snares-on velocity/round-robin grid.

## Inventory

Velocity-layer splits (`hivel` bounds) and round-robin counts were **parsed
from the source SFZ mappings** (`Programs/mappings/mid/*.sfz`), not guessed. A layer
covers `(previous hi + 1)..=hi` in MIDI velocity; the first starts at 0.

| Articulation | Files | Source stem | Vel layers (hivel) | RRs | Tail cap / fade |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| `ridebell` | 9 | `mid_ride_bell` | 3: ≤42, ≤85, ≤127 | 3 | 1.2 s / 0.30 s |
| `hhc` (hi-hat closed) | 16 | `mid_hh_closed` | 4: ≤31, ≤63, ≤95, ≤127 | 4 | 1.2 s / 0.25 s |

Total: 128 files. File naming: `<articulation>_vl{L}_rr{R}.wav`, 1-based,
e.g. `ride_vl2_rr3.wav`.

`*` — **adjacent-layer round robins.** The snare, cross-stick and toms have NO
true round robins at the source; instead they are DEEPLY velocity-layered
(36 / 16 / 16 single-take layers). Embedding
every layer would bloat the crate, so a subset of target layers is kept and
each target layer's round-robin slots are filled with ADJACENT source
velocity layers — distinct recorded takes within ~10 velocity points of each
other, timbrally near-identical once peak-normalized (the synth applies
velocity gain itself). The exact source-layer picks are the `vl_map` tables
in `prepare_drumkit.py` (snare: the top three source layers of each 6-layer
block, i.e. source vl 4-6/10-12/16-18/22-24/28-30/34-36 of 36). The `kick`
keeps the source's full 4×4 velocity×round-robin grid.

Notes:
- `hhh_vl3_rr1.flac` adds the source kit's independent `mid_hh_half_vl3_rr1.flac` Half-Open Hi-Hat take from the same pinned Virtuosity Drums revision. It is downmixed to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz, capped at 1.2 s and faded over the final 0.3 s.
- Extension v0.9.1 packages the complete prepared `vl3` round-robin sets used at runtime for closed (4), open (3), pedal (4), and half-open (4) Hi-Hat. It also packages four snare velocity layers (`vl3`–`vl6`) with three adjacent-take round robins per layer. All are from the same pinned CC0 Virtuosity Drums revision; the additional half-open takes receive the same mono 44.1 kHz, 1.2 s cap and 0.3 s tail-fade treatment as `hhh_vl3_rr1.flac`.
- Extension v0.9.3 replaces the four pedal files with the pinned source kit's drier `snaremic_hh_pedal_vl3_rr1`–`rr4` takes. They are downmixed to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz, high-passed at 500 Hz, capped at 0.5 s and faded from 0.28 s to remove the tonal low-frequency tail during repeated pedal notes.
- Tom fundamentals measured on the prepared samples (Goertzel over the
  settled 0.10–0.35 s window, stable within ±1 Hz across all takes/layers):
  `tomlo` ≈ 113.5 Hz, `tomhi` ≈ 181 Hz. The synth repitches these two drums
  across the six GM tom keys by playback rate.
- Virtuosity also has `hh_half`/`hh_34` (half / three-quarter open), a flat
  ride (`flatride_ride`, `flatride_crash`), and further snare articulations
  (rimshot, flam, buzz, rolls, stickshots); they were left out to keep the
  bank lean and can be added by extending `BANKS` / `PSEUDO_RR_BANKS` in
  `prepare_drumkit.py`.

## Processing (deterministic, mirrors `prepare.py`)

Per file: bit-exact FLAC decode (ffmpeg, `pcm_s24le`, native rate — ffmpeg is
tool-time only, the version does not affect the decoded PCM); stereo→mono
downmix (0.5/0.5); linear resample 48 kHz to 44.1 kHz; onset trim at 3% of peak with an 8 ms pre-pad; tail
cap per the table above with a squared fade-out; 2 ms fade-in; peak-normalize
to 0.9 (the bank-wide convention — per-layer natural loudness is discarded;
the synth applies velocity gain). Output: 16-bit mono WAV.

## Regenerating

```powershell
python3 tools/ferrosintesis-samples/prepare_drumkit.py
```

Pure-stdlib Python except the FLAC decode, which shells out to `ffmpeg` (must
be on PATH). Downloads are cached under the system temp directory keyed by
the pinned revisions and fetched atomically.
